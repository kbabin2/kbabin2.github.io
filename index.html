Rotom

A personal, self-hosted assistant.

Rotom is a private app used by a single person to manage their own Google Calendar. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

Rotom accesses Google Calendar data only for the account owner who authorized it, and only to read, create, and update calendar events at their request.

  • Data is processed on the owner's own hardware.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data is collected from anyone other than the account owner.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
